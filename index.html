Hello! My name is Aaron Kuang, and I am currently a senior at the Greenhill School, where I have studied at since fourth grade. Growing up in Texas with my family, including my two younger brothers, has shaped how I think about collaboration and responsibility. I especially love to play board games and basketball with my brothers (preferably indoors on sunny and hot days).

I am especially drawn to activities that reward strategy, logic, and preparation. Through debate, I’ve learned to enjoy traveling not just for competition, but for the opportunity to engage with unfamiliar perspectives alongside teammates and coaches who push me to think more deeply. Outside of debate, I love to fence and solve puzzles. Both of which appeal to me as they require patience and adaptability. A core memory of mine was winning the Hudson River Trading Puzzle Hunt during the Harvard-MIT Math Tournament! I also enjoy reading across genres, particularly critical theory such as psychoanalysis.

Ultimately, I hope to continue exploring ideas that sit at the intersection of strategy, analysis, and human behavior.
